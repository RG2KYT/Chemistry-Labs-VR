In VR (controllers or hand tracking)

  • Grab: squeeze the grip button / pinch your fingers (or make a fist) near an object. Point at far objects and grab to pull them to you.
  • Tap screens: touch them with your finger or controller tip, or point the laser and pull the trigger / pinch.
  • Move screens: grab the handle bar above or below a screen. Grab both bars to turn or resize it. Turn it 90° for portrait.
  • Build molecules: tap an element, tap + atom, then bring atoms close together while holding one — they snap into bonds.
  • Break bonds: hold two atoms of the same molecule (one in each hand) and pull them apart.
  • Synthesize: put a beaker in the bay of the machine, drop your molecule into the reactor on top — the real substance pours out of the tube.
  • Pour: tilt a container. Acids dissolve what they land on (it reforms a few seconds later).
  • Reset: hold the red button on the synthesizer for 2 seconds.
  • Controllers: left stick moves, right stick snap-turns (virtual lab only).

On a computer

  • Left-drag: grab / tap · mouse wheel: hold distance · Q/E and R/F: tilt what you hold (pour)
  • Shift + drag an atom: pull it out of its molecule · Right-drag or arrow keys: look · WASD: walk · Space: use tool
